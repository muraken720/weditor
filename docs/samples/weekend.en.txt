Weekend notes
=============

The city is quieter on Saturday mornings.
I like to take a walk before the streets get busy,
then stop at the bakery for fresh bread.

Things to bring
    - A small notebook
    - A bottle of water
    - A camera for the changing colors of the trees

Plans for the afternoon
1. Visit the bookshop near the station.
2. Try a new recipe for lunch.
3. Take another walk by the river.

Leave a little room for an unexpected discovery.
